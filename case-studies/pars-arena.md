# Case Study: Pars Arena Digital Commerce

[Back to profile](../README.md) · [Visit Pars Arena](https://parsarena.com)

## Context

Pars Arena is a technology e-commerce website built on WordPress and WooCommerce. The ongoing work combines site operations, product and editorial content, search visibility, customer journey improvement and practical automation.

This case study describes active workstreams without publishing private operational data or unverified performance claims.

## Areas of contribution

### SEO and information architecture

- Mapping search intent to product, category and editorial pages
- Improving page titles, descriptions, headings and internal linking
- Organizing content around technology products and customer questions
- Reviewing crawl behavior, indexability and duplicate URL patterns
- Using performance evidence to prioritize the next improvements

### Product and editorial content

- Building structured, readable product descriptions
- Creating comparison, specification, trust and FAQ sections
- Producing Persian technology-news and buying-guide content
- Maintaining source attribution and distinguishing verified facts from interpretation
- Designing reusable publishing structures instead of one-off pages

### WordPress and WooCommerce

- Implementing content and presentation improvements in WordPress
- Supporting WooCommerce catalog and product-page workflows
- Improving customer-facing guidance around ordering, payment and delivery
- Reviewing mobile readability, component consistency and conversion paths
- Keeping changes compatible with the site's operational environment

### E-commerce operations

- Improving catalog clarity and product discoverability
- Connecting content decisions with real customer questions
- Supporting the journey from search result to product evaluation and checkout
- Documenting repeatable workflows for future team use

### Responsible automation

The Pars Arena Newsbot project is being designed as a controlled editorial assistant rather than an autonomous publisher. Its planned safeguards include:

- approved source registry
- canonical URL tracking and duplicate prevention
- traceable source metadata
- structured Persian draft generation
- WordPress draft mode by default
- human review before public publishing
- secret separation and redacted logs

## Working approach

1. Identify the operational or customer-facing problem.
2. Gather evidence from the site, search data and user journey.
3. Make a scoped change that can be reviewed.
4. Verify readability, functionality and unintended effects.
5. Document the result and the next decision.
6. Publish performance claims only when supported by reliable measurements.

## Current status

The work is ongoing. Public case studies will be expanded when individual improvements have stable, shareable evidence. Internal credentials, customer data, security details and commercially sensitive metrics are intentionally excluded.
